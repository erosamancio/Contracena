## Platform

## Stack
delegated (inferred from brief, user not asked yet): static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS installable PWA (manifest + service worker), no build step, works offline, hostable on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify).

## Users
A couple in Brazil (the requester and his girlfriend), playing at home, usually at night, one phone passed between them or held by one person. Portuguese (pt-BR) only.

## Product Purpose
An improv scene drawer for two: each partner gets a distinct secret role and a shared situation, then they act it out as if it were real. Success = they laugh, play several rounds in a row, and come back on other nights without seeing repeats.

## Positioning
Two-sided scenes: every scene gives each partner a different role with a private objective that pulls against the other's, so the conflict writes itself. Recently played scenes are suppressed so repeats are rare.

## Operating Context
Living room / bedroom, phone in hand, partner reading the other's role aloud or passing the phone so each reads their own card privately. Short rounds (2-5 min). Inspired by an Instagram video of a couple acting out a burnt-dinner scene.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Large bank of scenes in pt-BR, each with: title, setting, role A, role B, secret objective per role, optional twist.
- Categories/modes chosen before drawing; anti-repeat history stored locally (localStorage).
- No accounts, no backend, offline after first load.
- Inferred, unconfirmed: content is playful/flirty but not explicit.

## Brand Commitments
Name is open (to be proposed). Requested writing style: natural, human Portuguese (humanizer skill), not AI-sounding.

## Product Principles
1. Get to acting in two taps.
2. Every role must be fun to play on its own, not just a straight man.
3. Never break the surprise: each partner's secret stays private until revealed.
4. Freshness over volume: the drawer actively avoids recent scenes.
